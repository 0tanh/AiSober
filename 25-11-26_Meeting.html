    • Goals

    What we are trying to achieve medium-long term

    We would like to deliberately foster an environment for technical geeks to get excited about technical nonsense. In the past there were many places that provided this, but as it stands many of these spaces are only focused on various interpreations of the few topics.

    If we have done our job, we will have a published series of high-quality technical talks, code examples and walkthroughs to serve as a reference for future engineers to use in their journey.

    Format

    Format of the Meetings i.e. what happens every meeting.

    Uoon arriving there is a 10-20 minute social warm-up period. Rules are already in effect If it is your first meeting, you are put in the list to do a talk in 2 meetings time.

    Meetings are formatted as a series of 10 minute lightning talks to start, Followed by currently unstructured discussion. These talks are then recorded, lightly edited and put on YouTube some time later in the month. This is not mandatory, but it is an option to available to speakers.

    There are rules on sign-up about what not to talk about. There is a per session leaderboard to keep track of who has brought up AI/Is in violation the most.

    If, at the end of the meeting, you have been found to have brought up AI the most, you are required to undergo a "punishment". Currently this is the form of having to do a timed leetcode hard problem with your editor being streamed, but this could take the form of other unpleasant technical challenges (write a particularly difficult regex etc.)

    Target Audience

    Who these meetings are for. Who we do and don't want there

    Who this is for

    A large number of technically inclined people feel culturally adrift and misaligned with the current AI zeitgeist. This is primarily designed as a productive meeting place for such people. All experience levels welcome, but this is perhaps most benefical to early career engineers & technical people.

    Our goal is to take pride in and reward attentive and detail oriented work, and provide an honest and earnest environment to get away from one of the biggest changes to the structure of how people interact with computers. The technical details do still matter, but we need to make them matter.

    This is heavily inspired by Don't Take the Black Pill by Andrew Kelly, the work of the Better Software Conference and Is There A Place in Tech for Me

    Who this isn't for

    From harshest to least harsh restriction

    1. No Military/No Facists/No Bigotry.

    2. If you want to talk about your agentic workflows, and your AI optimisation, you have every other platform available to you. Please not here. Good places in Cape Town to go include the Makers and Founders weekly meet-up, Indie Hackers and Claude coffee meet-ups We are deliberately aiming for a more narrow niche.

    3. As necessary as start-ups and industry have been to progressing software, this is also not the place to promote your business. If you feel you have solved your problem in an interesting and novel way, feel free to present! But our main goal is not to be a springboard for funding/financial motivations.

    Policies and Incentives

    Misc governance policies and how we plan to enforce them

    1. Worse is not Better

      We would like to champion high quality craftsmanship and thought over volume of output

    2. Long term craft

      Talks should aim to be as valuable 10 years from today, as they are now. Considered engineering and design never goes out of fashion.

    3. Presumption of Good Faith

      We want to try and provide a healthy social environment, so this means trying to foster a culture of curiousity and engaging in good faith.

      This means, avoiding trolling/ragebaiting/trying to be provocative or reactionary. We reserve the right to restrict access to this space someone is deliberately engaging in anti-social behaviour (being deliberately provocative/devil's advocating etc.)

    4. Pursuit of excellence

      We want to try to champion human achievement and excellence in a pro-social way. This means doing our best to highlight high quality work by humans who can care and think deeply about what they are designing

    5. Intellectual Honesty

      Learning is supposed to be challenging and frustrating. We are trying to provide a truly judgement free zone to those who earnestly are interested in improving the craft of writing and developing software.

      If we have done our job succesfully, a significant number of our lightning talks will meaningfully engage with learning from failure
